# Statement of Profit or Loss and Statement of Financial Position

> Excel file for this topic: `excel/Financial_Statements.xlsx` (full worked example plus a self-marking practice question)

## 1. Learning goals

1. Prepare a Statement of Profit or Loss for a sole trader.
2. Prepare a Statement of Financial Position for a sole trader.
3. Include year end adjustments from Chapters 8, 10 and 11.
4. Calculate working capital (net current assets).

## 2. The two statements

| Statement of Profit or Loss (SPL) | Statement of Financial Position (SFP) |
|---|---|
| Covers a **period**: "for the year ended 31 December 2025" | A **snapshot** on one day: "at 31 December 2025" |
| Shows income and expenses | Shows assets, liabilities and capital |
| Answers: did the business make a profit? | Answers: what does the business own and owe? |
| Closes income and expense accounts | Lists balances which carry forward to next year |

**Key formulas**

* Net revenue = Revenue - Sales returns
* Cost of sales = Opening inventory + Purchases - Purchases returns + Carriage inwards - Closing inventory
* Gross profit = Net revenue - Cost of sales
* Profit for the year = Gross profit + Other income - Expenses

**Carriage inwards vs carriage outwards**

* Carriage **inwards** is the cost of bringing goods **in** from suppliers. It is part of **cost of sales**.
* Carriage **outwards** is the cost of delivering goods **out** to customers. It is an **expense**.

## 5. How to deal with each adjustment

| Adjustment | In the SPL | In the SFP |
|---|---|---|
| Closing inventory | Deduct in cost of sales | Current asset |
| Accrued expense | Add to the expense | Current liability (other payables) |
| Prepaid expense | Deduct from the expense | Current asset (other receivables) |
| Accrued income | Add to the income | Current asset (other receivables) |
| Income received in advance | Deduct from the income | Current liability (other payables) |
| Irrecoverable debt written off (after trial balance) | Add to irrecoverable debts expense | Deduct from trade receivables |
| Increase in allowance | Expense | Receivables less **new** allowance |
| Decrease in allowance | Other income | Receivables less **new** allowance |
| Depreciation for the year | Expense | Add to accumulated depreciation |
| Loan interest owing | Expense | Current liability (other payables) |

**Golden rule:** every adjustment affects **both** statements. Tick each adjustment twice as you use it.

## 7. Exam tips

* Write the **full heading**: business name, statement name, date or period.
* Show **workings** for every adjusted figure. Examiners give marks for method.
* Tick each trial balance item as you use it. Every item is used **once**.
* Drawings go in the **capital** section, never in the SPL.
* A loan repayable within 12 months is a **current** liability. After 12 months it is **non-current**.
* A bank overdraft is a current liability.

## 8. Common mistakes

* Putting carriage inwards under expenses.
* Using the trial balance allowance instead of the **new** allowance in the SFP.
* Adding the whole allowance to expenses instead of only the change.
* Forgetting loan interest owing.
* Treating drawings as an expense.
