# Glossary: Key Terms in English and Chinese

Key accounting terms for Chapters 8, 10, 11 and 12 and the financial statements.

> The Chinese terms are common Simplified Chinese equivalents. Chinese-medium textbooks sometimes use different terms, so check them against your school's textbook and use your teacher's terms in tests.

## General and financial statements

| English | 中文 | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statement of Profit or Loss (SPL) | 损益表 | Shows income, expenses and profit for a period | FS |
| Statement of Financial Position (SOFP) | 财务状况表 | Shows assets, liabilities and capital on one date | FS |
| Journal | 日记账 | Book of first entry where transactions are recorded in date order | All |
| Ledger | 分类账 | Book containing all the accounts | All |
| Folio | 账页 / 过账页码 | Reference to the page of the other book | All |
| Narrative | 摘要说明 | Short explanation under a journal entry | All |
| Balance carried down (c/d) | 余额结转下期 | Balance taken to the next period | All |
| Balance brought down (b/d) | 余额承前 | Balance brought from the last period | All |
| Trial balance | 试算表 | List of all balances to check debits equal credits | FS |
| Revenue | 营业收入 | Income from selling goods or services | FS |
| Sales returns | 销货退回 | Goods returned by customers | FS |
| Purchases returns | 进货退出 | Goods returned to suppliers | FS |
| Cost of sales | 销货成本 | Cost of the goods that were sold | FS |
| Gross profit | 毛利 | Net revenue minus cost of sales | FS |
| Profit for the year | 本年利润 | Gross profit plus other income minus expenses | FS |
| Carriage inwards | 进货运费 | Cost of bringing goods in from suppliers | FS |
| Carriage outwards | 销货运费 | Cost of delivering goods to customers | FS |
| Discount allowed | 销货折扣 | Discount given to customers for paying early | FS |
| Discount received | 进货折扣 | Discount received from suppliers for paying early | FS |
| Inventory | 存货 | Goods held for resale | FS |
| Capital | 资本 | Owner's investment in the business | FS |
| Drawings | 提款 | Money or goods taken by the owner for private use | FS |
| Working capital | 营运资金 | Current assets minus current liabilities | FS |
| Net assets | 净资产 | Total assets minus total liabilities | FS |
| Bank overdraft | 银行透支 | Bank balance owed to the bank | FS |
| Loan | 贷款 | Money borrowed, to be repaid later | FS |

## Chapter 8: Irrecoverable debts

| English | 中文 | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade receivables | 应收账款 | Credit customers who owe the business money | 8 |
| Trade payables | 应付账款 | Credit suppliers the business owes money to | 8 |
| Irrecoverable debt | 坏账 | A debt that will never be collected | 8 |
| Write off | 冲销 / 注销 | Remove a debt from the customer's account as an expense | 8 |
| Irrecoverable debts recovered | 坏账收回 | A debt written off earlier that is later paid | 8 |
| Allowance for irrecoverable debts | 坏账准备 | Estimate of debts that might not be paid | 8 |
| Bankrupt | 破产 | Unable to pay debts | 8 |

## Chapter 10: Non-current assets and depreciation

| English | 中文 | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-current assets | 非流动资产 | Assets used in the business for more than one year | 10 |
| Current assets | 流动资产 | Assets expected to be used or turned into cash within one year | 10, FS |
| Tangible asset | 有形资产 | An asset you can touch | 10 |
| Intangible asset | 无形资产 | An asset you cannot touch, such as goodwill | 10 |
| Capital expenditure | 资本支出 | Spending on buying or improving non-current assets | 10 |
| Revenue expenditure | 收益支出 | Day to day running costs | 10 |
| Depreciation | 折旧 | Part of an asset's cost used up in a period | 10 |
| Provision for depreciation (accumulated depreciation) | 累计折旧 | Total depreciation charged since the asset was bought | 10 |
| Carrying amount (net book value) | 账面价值 | Cost minus accumulated depreciation | 10 |
| Residual value | 残值 | Expected value at the end of the asset's useful life | 10 |
| Useful life | 使用年限 | Number of years the asset is expected to be used | 10 |
| Straight line method | 直线法 | Same depreciation charge every year | 10 |
| Reducing balance method | 余额递减法 | Fixed percentage of the carrying amount each year | 10 |
| Revaluation method | 重估法 | Opening value plus purchases minus closing value | 10 |
| Obsolescence | 过时 / 陈旧 | Becoming out of date | 10 |
| Wear and tear | 损耗 | Loss of value through use | 10 |
| Disposal | 处置 | Selling or scrapping a non-current asset | 10 |
| Profit on disposal | 处置利润 | Sale proceeds greater than carrying amount | 10 |
| Loss on disposal | 处置损失 | Sale proceeds less than carrying amount | 10 |

## Chapter 11: Accruals and prepayments

| English | 中文 | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accrued expense (accrual) | 应计费用 | Expense used but not yet paid | 11 |
| Prepaid expense (prepayment) | 预付费用 | Expense paid in advance for the next period | 11 |
| Accrued income | 应计收入 | Income earned but not yet received | 11 |
| Income received in advance | 预收收入 | Income received for a future period | 11 |
| Other receivables | 其他应收款 | Prepayments and accrued income in the SOFP | 11 |
| Other payables | 其他应付款 | Accruals and income in advance in the SOFP | 11 |
| Current liabilities | 流动负债 | Amounts due within one year | 11, FS |
| Non-current liabilities | 非流动负债 | Amounts due after more than one year | FS |

## Chapter 12: Accounting concepts

| English | 中文 | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business entity concept | 企业个体概念 | The business is separate from its owner | 12 |
| Duality concept | 二重性概念 / 复式记账 | Every transaction has two effects | 12 |
| Money measurement concept | 货币计量概念 | Only items with a money value are recorded | 12 |
| Historical cost concept | 历史成本概念 | Assets are recorded at their original cost | 12 |
| Going concern concept | 持续经营概念 | The business will continue trading | 12 |
| Realisation concept | 实现概念 | Revenue is recorded when it is earned | 12 |
| Accruals (matching) concept | 应计 (配比) 概念 | Income and expenses are matched to their period | 12 |
| Consistency concept | 一致性概念 | The same methods are used every year | 12 |
| Prudence concept | 谨慎性概念 | Assets and profit are not overstated | 12 |
| Materiality concept | 重要性概念 | Small items can be treated simply | 12 |
| Net realisable value (NRV) | 可变现净值 | Selling price minus costs to sell or repair | 12 |
| Relevance | 相关性 | Information helps users make decisions | 12 |
| Reliability | 可靠性 | Information is free from errors and bias | 12 |
| Comparability | 可比性 | Information can be compared across years and businesses | 12 |
| Understandability | 可理解性 | Information is clear to users | 12 |
