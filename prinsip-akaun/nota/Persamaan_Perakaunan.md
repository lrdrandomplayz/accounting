## 2. Istilah penting (Key terms)

| Istilah (Term) | Maksud | Meaning in English | Contoh (Examples) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aset (Assets) | Sumber ekonomi yang dimiliki dan dikawal oleh perniagaan dan memberi faedah pada masa hadapan | Resources the business owns and controls that give future benefits | Premis, kenderaan, perabot, inventori, Akaun Belum Terima, bank, tunai *(premises, vehicles, furniture, inventory, trade receivables, bank, cash)* |
| Liabiliti (Liabilities) | Tanggungan atau hutang perniagaan kepada pihak luar | Debts the business owes to outsiders | Akaun Belum Bayar, pinjaman bank, overdraf bank, belanja belum bayar *(trade payables, bank loan, bank overdraft, accrued expenses)* |
| Ekuiti pemilik (Owner's equity) | Tuntutan pemilik ke atas aset perniagaan selepas ditolak liabiliti | The owner's claim on the assets after liabilities are taken away | Modal, untung bersih (tolak ambilan) *(capital, net profit, less drawings)* |
| Hasil (Revenue / income) | Pendapatan perniagaan yang menambah ekuiti pemilik | Income that increases owner's equity | Jualan, komisen diterima, sewa diterima *(sales, commission received, rent received)* |
| Belanja (Expenses) | Kos menjalankan perniagaan yang mengurangkan ekuiti pemilik | Running costs that decrease owner's equity | Gaji, sewa, kadar bayaran, elektrik *(wages, rent, rates, electricity)* |
| Ambilan (Drawings) | Wang atau barang yang diambil oleh pemilik untuk kegunaan sendiri. Mengurangkan ekuiti pemilik | Money or goods the owner takes for private use. Decreases owner's equity | Pemilik mengambil tunai *(owner takes cash)* |

## 3. Persamaan perakaunan (The accounting equation)

> **Aset = Liabiliti + Ekuiti Pemilik**
>
> ***Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity***

Persamaan ini sentiasa seimbang kerana setiap aset dibiayai sama ada oleh pihak luar (liabiliti) atau oleh pemilik (ekuiti pemilik).

*The equation always balances because every asset is paid for either by outsiders (liabilities) or by the owner (owner's equity).*

**Bentuk lain persamaan (Other forms of the equation):**

* Ekuiti Pemilik = Aset − Liabiliti *(Owner's equity = Assets − Liabilities)*
* Liabiliti = Aset − Ekuiti Pemilik *(Liabilities = Assets − Owner's equity)*

**Persamaan perakaunan yang dikembangkan (The expanded accounting equation):**

> **Aset = Liabiliti + Modal + Hasil − Belanja − Ambilan**
>
> ***Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Income − Expenses − Drawings***

* Hasil − Belanja = Untung bersih (atau rugi bersih jika belanja lebih besar). *Income − Expenses = Net profit (or net loss if expenses are bigger).*
* Ekuiti pemilik akhir = Modal awal + Modal tambahan + Untung bersih − Ambilan. *Closing owner's equity = Opening capital + Additional capital + Net profit − Drawings.*

## 4. Kesan urus niaga (Effects of transactions)

Setiap urus niaga memberi kesan kepada **sekurang-kurangnya dua item**. Selepas setiap urus niaga, jumlah aset tetap sama dengan jumlah liabiliti dan ekuiti pemilik.

*Every transaction affects **at least two items**. After each transaction, total assets still equal total liabilities plus owner's equity.*

Kunci (Key): Bertambah = *increase*. Berkurang = *decrease*. Tiada kesan = *no effect*.

| Urus niaga (Transaction) | Aset (Assets) | Liabiliti (Liabilities) | Ekuiti pemilik (Owner's equity) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pemilik memulakan perniagaan dengan wang tunai *(owner starts the business with cash)* | Bertambah (tunai/bank) | Tiada kesan | Bertambah (modal) |
| Membeli aset secara tunai atau cek *(buy an asset by cash or cheque)* | Bertambah dan berkurang (jumlah aset tidak berubah) *(one up, one down, total unchanged)* | Tiada kesan | Tiada kesan |
| Membeli aset atau barang niaga secara kredit *(buy an asset or goods on credit)* | Bertambah | Bertambah | Tiada kesan |
| Menjual barang niaga dengan untung *(sell goods at a profit)* | Bertambah (kos keluar, harga jualan masuk) *(cost out, selling price in)* | Tiada kesan | Bertambah (untung) *(profit)* |
| Menerima pinjaman *(receive a loan)* | Bertambah | Bertambah | Tiada kesan |
| Membayar belanja (gaji, sewa) *(pay an expense: wages, rent)* | Berkurang | Tiada kesan | Berkurang |
| Menerima hasil (komisen, sewa) *(receive income: commission, rent)* | Bertambah | Tiada kesan | Bertambah |
| Membayar Akaun Belum Bayar *(pay a trade payable)* | Berkurang | Berkurang | Tiada kesan |
| Membayar Akaun Belum Bayar dan menerima diskaun *(pay a trade payable and receive a discount)* | Berkurang (amaun dibayar) *(amount paid)* | Berkurang (amaun penuh) *(full amount)* | Bertambah (diskaun diterima) *(discount received)* |
| Menerima bayaran daripada Akaun Belum Terima *(receive payment from a trade receivable)* | Bertambah dan berkurang | Tiada kesan | Tiada kesan |
| Pemilik membuat ambilan *(owner takes drawings)* | Berkurang | Tiada kesan | Berkurang |
| Pemilik menambah modal *(owner adds capital)* | Bertambah | Tiada kesan | Bertambah |

**Tip:** diskaun diterima ialah hasil, jadi ekuiti pemilik bertambah. Diskaun diberi ialah belanja, jadi ekuiti pemilik berkurang.

*Tip: discount received is income, so owner's equity increases. Discount allowed is an expense, so owner's equity decreases.*

## 7. Menghitung item yang tidak diketahui (Calculating a missing item)

**Contoh 1 (Example 1):** Aset RM96 500, liabiliti RM31 200. *Assets RM96 500, liabilities RM31 200.*

Ekuiti pemilik = 96 500 − 31 200 = **RM65 300** *(Owner's equity = assets − liabilities)*

**Contoh 2 (Example 2):** Modal awal RM48 000, modal tambahan RM5 000, hasil RM36 500, belanja RM21 800, ambilan RM6 200.

*Opening capital RM48 000, additional capital RM5 000, income RM36 500, expenses RM21 800, drawings RM6 200.*

* Untung bersih = 36 500 − 21 800 = RM14 700 *(Net profit = income − expenses)*
* Ekuiti pemilik akhir = 48 000 + 5 000 + 14 700 − 6 200 = **RM61 500** *(Closing owner's equity)*

## 8. Kesilapan biasa (Common mistakes)

* Menganggap pembelian aset secara tunai menambah jumlah aset. Satu aset bertambah dan satu lagi berkurang dengan jumlah yang sama.
  *Thinking a cash purchase of an asset raises total assets. One asset goes up and another goes down by the same amount.*
* Lupa kesan diskaun: liabiliti berkurang dengan **amaun penuh**, aset berkurang dengan **amaun yang dibayar**.
  *Forgetting the discount: liabilities fall by the **full amount**, assets fall by the **amount paid**.*
* Menganggap ambilan sebagai belanja. Ambilan mengurangkan ekuiti pemilik tetapi bukan belanja perniagaan.
  *Treating drawings as an expense. Drawings reduce owner's equity, but they are not a business expense.*
* Merekod keseluruhan harga jualan sebagai untung. Untung ialah harga jualan tolak kos.
  *Recording the whole selling price as profit. Profit is selling price minus cost.*

## 9. Semak kefahaman (Check your understanding)

1. Pemilik memasukkan wang peribadi RM8 000 ke dalam bank perniagaan. Nyatakan kesannya.
   *The owner puts RM8 000 of personal money into the business bank account. State the effect.*
2. Aset RM120 000 dan ekuiti pemilik RM85 000. Berapakah liabiliti?
   *Assets are RM120 000 and owner's equity is RM85 000. What are the liabilities?*
3. Barang niaga berkos RM900 dijual secara tunai dengan harga RM1 300. Nyatakan kesannya.
   *Goods costing RM900 are sold for cash at RM1 300. State the effect.*

Jawapan (Answers):

1. Aset bertambah RM8 000, ekuiti pemilik bertambah RM8 000. *Assets increase by RM8 000, owner's equity increases by RM8 000.*
2. RM35 000.
3. Aset bertambah RM400 (tunai +1 300, inventori −900), ekuiti pemilik bertambah RM400. *Assets increase by RM400 (cash +1 300, inventory −900), owner's equity increases by RM400.*
