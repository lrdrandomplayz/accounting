# Glosari Prinsip Akaun (Glossary: Malay, English, Chinese)

Istilah penting bagi Persamaan Perakaunan, Lejar dan Akaun Kawalan. *Key terms for the accounting equation, ledgers and control accounts.*

> The Chinese terms are common Simplified Chinese equivalents. Chinese-medium textbooks sometimes use different terms, so check them against your school's textbook and use your teacher's terms in tests. In SPM, always write the Malay term.

## Persamaan Perakaunan (Accounting equation)

| Bahasa Melayu | English | 中文 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persamaan perakaunan | Accounting equation | 会计等式 | Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity |
| Aset | Assets | 资产 | Resources the business owns and controls |
| Aset bukan semasa | Non-current assets | 非流动资产 | Assets kept for more than one year |
| Aset semasa | Current assets | 流动资产 | Assets used or turned into cash within one year |
| Liabiliti | Liabilities | 负债 | Debts owed to outsiders |
| Liabiliti bukan semasa | Non-current liabilities | 非流动负债 | Debts due after more than one year |
| Liabiliti semasa | Current liabilities | 流动负债 | Debts due within one year |
| Ekuiti pemilik | Owner's equity | 业主权益 | Assets minus liabilities, the owner's claim |
| Modal | Capital | 资本 | Money or assets the owner puts into the business |
| Modal tambahan | Additional capital | 追加资本 | Extra capital added later |
| Ambilan | Drawings | 提款 | Money or goods the owner takes for private use |
| Hasil | Income / revenue | 收入 | Earnings that increase owner's equity |
| Belanja | Expenses | 费用 | Running costs that decrease owner's equity |
| Untung bersih | Net profit | 净利 | Income minus expenses |
| Rugi bersih | Net loss | 净亏损 | Expenses greater than income |
| Urus niaga | Transaction | 交易 | A business event with a money value |
| Barang niaga | Goods (merchandise) | 商品 | Goods bought for resale |
| Inventori | Inventory | 存货 | Goods held for resale |
| Tunai | Cash | 现金 | Notes and coins |
| Bank | Bank | 银行存款 | Money in the business bank account |
| Pinjaman | Loan | 贷款 | Money borrowed, to be repaid later |
| Overdraf bank | Bank overdraft | 银行透支 | Bank balance owed to the bank |
| Perabot | Furniture | 家具 | A non-current asset |
| Kenderaan | Motor vehicle | 汽车 | A non-current asset |
| Premis | Premises | 房产 | Land and buildings used by the business |
| Penyata Kedudukan Kewangan | Statement of Financial Position | 财务状况表 | Shows assets, liabilities and owner's equity on one date |
| Bertambah | Increase | 增加 | Goes up |
| Berkurang | Decrease | 减少 | Goes down |
| Tiada kesan | No effect | 无影响 | Does not change |

## Lejar (Ledgers)

| Bahasa Melayu | English | 中文 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lejar | Ledger | 分类账 | Book of final entry containing all the accounts |
| Lejar Am | General Ledger | 总分类账 | All accounts except individual customers and suppliers |
| Lejar Khas (Lejar Subsidiari) | Special (subsidiary) ledgers | 明细分类账 | The Sales Ledger and the Purchases Ledger |
| Lejar Jualan | Sales Ledger | 销货分类账 | One account for each credit customer |
| Lejar Belian | Purchases Ledger | 进货分类账 | One account for each credit supplier |
| Sistem Catatan Bergu | Double entry system | 复式记账法 | Every transaction is debited and credited |
| Debit (Dt) | Debit (Dr) | 借方 | Left side of an account |
| Kredit (Kt) | Credit (Cr) | 贷方 | Right side of an account |
| Bentuk T | T form | T字账 | Ledger with a debit side and a credit side |
| Bentuk berbaki | Running balance form | 余额式账户 | Ledger with a balance column updated after each entry |
| Tarikh | Date | 日期 | Column for the date |
| Butir | Particulars | 摘要 | Column naming the other account |
| Folio | Folio | 账页 | Reference to the book of first entry |
| Amaun | Amount | 金额 | Column for the money value |
| Baki | Balance | 余额 | Difference between the two sides |
| Baki b/b (bawa turun) | Balance b/d (brought down) | 承前余额 | Balance at the start of the period |
| Baki h/b (hantar bawah) | Balance c/d (carried down) | 结转余额 | Balance at the end of the period |
| Angka pengimbang | Balancing figure | 平衡数 | Amount that makes both sides equal |
| Akaun nyata | Real account | 实账户 | Account for assets, liabilities and owner's equity |
| Akaun nominal | Nominal account | 虚账户 | Account for income and expenses |
| Akaun Untung Rugi | Profit or Loss account | 损益账 | Where nominal accounts close at year end |
| Imbangan duga | Trial balance | 试算表 | List of balances to check debits equal credits |

## Akaun Kawalan (Control accounts)

| Bahasa Melayu | English | 中文 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Akaun kawalan | Control account | 控制账户 | Total account for a special ledger, kept in the General Ledger |
| Akaun Kawalan Belum Terima (AKBT) | Trade receivables control account | 应收账款控制账户 | Total of all customer accounts |
| Akaun Kawalan Belum Bayar (AKBB) | Trade payables control account | 应付账款控制账户 | Total of all supplier accounts |
| Akaun Belum Terima | Trade receivables | 应收账款 | Credit customers who owe the business |
| Akaun Belum Bayar | Trade payables | 应付账款 | Credit suppliers the business owes |
| Pelanggan | Customer | 顾客 | Person who buys from the business |
| Pembekal | Supplier | 供应商 | Person who sells to the business |
| Jualan kredit | Credit sales | 赊销 | Sales paid for later |
| Belian kredit | Credit purchases | 赊购 | Purchases paid for later |
| Pulangan jualan | Sales returns (returns inwards) | 销货退回 | Goods returned by customers |
| Pulangan belian | Purchases returns (returns outwards) | 进货退出 | Goods returned to suppliers |
| Diskaun diberi | Discount allowed | 销货折扣 | Discount given to customers |
| Diskaun diterima | Discount received | 进货折扣 | Discount received from suppliers |
| Hutang lapuk | Bad debts (irrecoverable debts) | 烂账 | Debt that will never be collected |
| Peruntukan hutang ragu | Allowance for doubtful debts | 烂账准备 | Estimate of debts that might not be paid. Not in control accounts |
| Cek tak laku | Dishonoured cheque | 退票 | Cheque the bank refuses to pay |
| Faedah | Interest | 利息 | Charge on an overdue account |
| Angkutan masuk | Carriage inwards | 进货运费 | Delivery cost on goods bought |
| Angkutan keluar | Carriage outwards | 销货运费 | Delivery cost on goods sold |
| Kontra (catatan kontra) | Contra (set-off) entry | 对销 / 抵销 | Smaller balance set off between the Sales and Purchases Ledgers |
| Elaun | Allowance | 折让 | Price reduction given or received |
| Terlebih bayar | Overpayment | 多付 | Paying more than the amount owed |
| Penyelewengan | Fraud | 舞弊 | Dishonest misuse of money or records |
| Kesilapan ketinggalan catatan | Error of omission | 遗漏错误 | Transaction not recorded at all |
| Implikasi | Limitations | 局限 | What a control account cannot detect |
| Sumber maklumat | Source of information | 资料来源 | Book the figure comes from |
