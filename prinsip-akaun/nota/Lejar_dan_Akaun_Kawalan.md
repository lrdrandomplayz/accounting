## 2. Lejar (The ledger)

**Lejar** ialah buku catatan akhir yang merekodkan semua urus niaga perniagaan mengikut Sistem Catatan Bergu (setiap urus niaga didebitkan dan dikreditkan).

*The **ledger** is the book of final entry. It records every business transaction using the double entry system: each transaction is debited and credited.*

| Jenis lejar (Type of ledger) | Kandungan | Contents in English |
|---|---|---|
| **Lejar Am** (General Ledger) | Semua akaun selain Akaun Belum Terima dan Akaun Belum Bayar: aset, liabiliti, ekuiti pemilik, hasil dan belanja | All accounts except trade receivables and trade payables: assets, liabilities, owner's equity, income and expenses |
| **Lejar Jualan** (Sales Ledger), juga Lejar Akaun Belum Terima | Akaun setiap pelanggan yang membeli barang niaga secara kredit | One account for each credit customer |
| **Lejar Belian** (Purchases Ledger), juga Lejar Akaun Belum Bayar | Akaun setiap pembekal yang menjual barang niaga secara kredit | One account for each credit supplier |

Lejar Jualan dan Lejar Belian dikenali sebagai **Lejar Khas** (Lejar Subsidiari).

*The Sales Ledger and the Purchases Ledger are called **special ledgers** (subsidiary ledgers).*

## 3. Bentuk lejar (Ledger formats)

**(a) Bentuk T (T form):** sebelah debit (Dt) di kiri dan sebelah kredit (Kt) di kanan. Setiap sebelah mempunyai lajur Tarikh | Butir | Folio | Amaun. Baris pertama di bawah tajuk menunjukkan tahun, dengan RM dalam lajur Amaun.

*Debit side (Dt) on the left, credit side (Kt) on the right. Each side has the columns Date | Particulars | Folio | Amount. The first row under the title shows the year, with RM in the Amount column.*

**(b) Bentuk berbaki (Running balance form):** satu jadual dengan lajur Tarikh | Butir | Folio | Debit | Kredit | Baki. Baki dikira selepas setiap catatan. Bentuk ini sering digunakan untuk akaun pelanggan dan pembekal kerana baki terkini mudah dilihat.

*One table with the columns Date | Particulars | Folio | Debit | Credit | Balance. The balance is worked out after every entry. Businesses often use this form for customer and supplier accounts because the latest balance is easy to see.*

## 4. Akaun nyata dan akaun nominal (Real and nominal accounts)

| Akaun nyata (Real accounts) | Akaun nominal (Nominal accounts) |
|---|---|
| Merekodkan semua aset, liabiliti dan ekuiti pemilik. *Record all assets, liabilities and owner's equity.* | Merekodkan semua hasil dan belanja. *Record all income and expenses.* |
| Baki dibawa ke tempoh berikutnya. *The balance carries on to the next period.* | Ditutup ke Akaun Untung Rugi pada akhir tempoh. *Closed to the Profit or Loss account at the end of the period.* |
| Contoh: premis, perabot, kenderaan, bank, tunai, inventori, Akaun Belum Bayar, modal. *Premises, furniture, vehicles, bank, cash, inventory, trade payables, capital.* | Contoh: pulangan jualan, pulangan belian, insurans, sewa, komisen diterima, jualan. *Sales returns, purchases returns, insurance, rent, commission received, sales.* |

**Peraturan merekod (Recording rules):**

| Jenis akaun (Type of account) | Bertambah (Increase) | Berkurang (Decrease) |
|---|---|---|
| Aset (Assets) | Debit | Kredit (Credit) |
| Belanja dan ambilan (Expenses and drawings) | Debit | Kredit (Credit) |
| Liabiliti (Liabilities) | Kredit (Credit) | Debit |
| Ekuiti pemilik, modal (Owner's equity, capital) | Kredit (Credit) | Debit |
| Hasil (Income) | Kredit (Credit) | Debit |

## 5. Akaun Kawalan (Control accounts)

### 5.1 Tujuan (fungsi) akaun kawalan (Purposes of control accounts)

Akaun kawalan disediakan dalam Lejar Am untuk menunjukkan **jumlah baki** semua Akaun Belum Terima atau semua Akaun Belum Bayar.

*A control account sits in the General Ledger. It shows the **total balance** of all trade receivables, or of all trade payables.*

1. Memudahkan mengesan kesilapan dalam lejar khas. *Makes errors in the special ledgers easier to find.*
2. Menyemak ketepatan catatan dalam Lejar Jualan dan Lejar Belian. *Checks the accuracy of the Sales Ledger and Purchases Ledger.*
3. Memudahkan penyediaan imbangan duga, kerana hanya satu jumlah digunakan bagi setiap lejar khas. *Makes the trial balance quicker, because each special ledger needs only one total.*
4. Mencegah dan mengesan penyelewengan. *Prevents and detects fraud.*

Terdapat dua akaun kawalan: *There are two control accounts:*

* **Akaun Kawalan Belum Terima** (Akaun Kawalan Lejar Jualan): mewakili semua Akaun Belum Terima. *Trade receivables control account (sales ledger control account): stands for all trade receivables.*
* **Akaun Kawalan Belum Bayar** (Akaun Kawalan Lejar Belian): mewakili semua Akaun Belum Bayar. *Trade payables control account (purchases ledger control account): stands for all trade payables.*

### 5.2 Akaun Kawalan Belum Terima (Trade receivables control account)

| Sebelah debit (Debit side) | Sebelah kredit (Credit side) |
|---|---|
| Baki b/b, baki debit awal *(opening debit balance)* | Baki b/b, baki kredit awal, jika ada *(opening credit balance, if any)* |
| Jualan kredit *(credit sales)* | Bank: tunai dan cek diterima *(cash and cheques received)* |
| Bank: cek tak laku *(dishonoured cheques)* | Diskaun diberi *(discount allowed)* |
| Diskaun diberi dibatalkan *(discount allowed cancelled)* | Pulangan jualan *(sales returns, returns inwards)* |
| Faedah atas akaun tertunggak *(interest on overdue accounts)* | Hutang lapuk *(bad debts written off)* |
| Angkutan keluar dicaj kepada pelanggan *(carriage outwards charged to customers)* | Kontra *(contra)* |
| Baki h/b, baki kredit akhir, jika ada *(closing credit balance, if any)* | Baki h/b, baki debit akhir *(closing debit balance)* |

**Sumber maklumat (Sources of information):** Jurnal Jualan, Jurnal Pulangan Jualan, Buku Tunai, Jurnal Am. *Sales Journal, Sales Returns Journal, Cash Book, General Journal.*

**Berbaki kredit jika (Has a credit balance when):** pelanggan terlebih bayar, pelanggan memulangkan barang niaga selepas hutang dijelaskan, atau elaun atau diskaun diberi selepas hutang dijelaskan.

*A customer overpays, a customer returns goods after paying in full, or an allowance or discount is given after the debt is paid.*

### 5.3 Akaun Kawalan Belum Bayar (Trade payables control account)

| Sebelah debit (Debit side) | Sebelah kredit (Credit side) |
|---|---|
| Baki b/b, baki debit awal, jika ada *(opening debit balance, if any)* | Baki b/b, baki kredit awal *(opening credit balance)* |
| Bank: tunai dan cek dibayar *(cash and cheques paid)* | Belian kredit *(credit purchases)* |
| Diskaun diterima *(discount received)* | Angkutan masuk dicaj oleh pembekal *(carriage inwards charged by suppliers)* |
| Pulangan belian *(purchases returns, returns outwards)* | Faedah dicaj oleh pembekal *(interest charged by suppliers)* |
| Kontra *(contra)* | Baki h/b, baki debit akhir, jika ada *(closing debit balance, if any)* |
| Baki h/b, baki kredit akhir *(closing credit balance)* | |

**Sumber maklumat (Sources of information):** Jurnal Belian, Jurnal Pulangan Belian, Buku Tunai, Jurnal Am. *Purchases Journal, Purchases Returns Journal, Cash Book, General Journal.*

**Berbaki debit jika (Has a debit balance when):** perniagaan terlebih bayar kepada pembekal, perniagaan memulangkan barang niaga selepas hutang dijelaskan, atau elaun atau diskaun diterima selepas hutang dijelaskan.

*The business overpays a supplier, returns goods after paying in full, or receives an allowance or discount after the debt is paid.*

**Langkah menyediakan akaun kawalan (Steps to prepare a control account):**

1. Catat Baki b/b pada hari pertama tempoh. *Enter the balance b/d on the first day of the period.*
2. Catat setiap item di sebelah yang betul (rujuk jadual 5.2 dan 5.3). *Enter each item on the correct side (see tables 5.2 and 5.3).*
3. Hitung Baki h/b sebagai angka pengimbang: jumlah sebelah yang lebih besar tolak semua item lain di sebelah yang lebih kecil. *Work out the balance c/d as the balancing figure: the bigger side's total minus every other item on the smaller side.*
4. Jumlahkan kedua-dua sebelah. Jumlahnya mesti sama. *Total both sides. The totals must match.*
5. Bawa turun baki pada hari pertama tempoh berikutnya, di sebelah yang bertentangan. *Bring the balance down on the first day of the next period, on the opposite side.*

**Jika soalan tidak memberi jualan atau belian kredit**, hitung sebagai angka pengimbang menggunakan baki awal dan baki akhir yang diberi (lihat Soalan 3).

***If the question does not give credit sales or credit purchases**, work them out as the balancing figure from the opening and closing balances given (see Question 3).*

## 6. Catatan kontra (Contra entries)

1. Catatan kontra ialah pindahan daripada Lejar Jualan ke Lejar Belian atau sebaliknya. *A contra entry is a transfer between the Sales Ledger and the Purchases Ledger.*
2. Hanya berlaku apabila sebuah perniagaan menjual dan juga membeli barang niaga secara kredit daripada peniaga yang sama. *It only happens when the business both sells to and buys from the same trader on credit.*
3. Baki akaun yang lebih kecil dipindahkan ke baki akaun yang lebih besar. *The smaller balance is set off against the bigger balance.*

**Contoh (Example):** pada 3 Januari 2016, Perniagaan Wawan membeli barang niaga RM1 500 daripada Pembekal Yani. Pada 5 Januari 2016, Perniagaan Wawan menjual barang niaga RM450 kepada Pembekal Yani. Baki yang lebih kecil, RM450, dikontrakan.

*On 3 January 2016, Perniagaan Wawan bought goods for RM1 500 from Pembekal Yani. On 5 January 2016, Perniagaan Wawan sold goods for RM450 to Pembekal Yani. The smaller balance, RM450, is the contra.*

## 7. Implikasi akaun kawalan (Limitations of control accounts)

Akaun kawalan hanya menyemak jumlah. Oleh itu: *A control account only checks totals. So:*

1. Sukar mengesan kesilapan ketinggalan catatan. *Errors of omission are hard to find.*
2. Sukar mengesan kesilapan menjumlahkan Akaun Belum Terima dan Akaun Belum Bayar. *Adding errors in the trade receivables and trade payables accounts are hard to find.*
3. Sukar mengesan kesilapan memindahkan catatan. *Posting errors are hard to find.*

## 8. Kesilapan biasa (Common mistakes)

* Merekod jualan tunai dalam Akaun Kawalan Belum Terima. Hanya jualan **kredit** dimasukkan.
  *Putting cash sales in the trade receivables control account. Only **credit** sales go in.*
* Meletakkan hutang lapuk di sebelah debit. Hutang lapuk mengurangkan Akaun Belum Terima, jadi di sebelah **kredit**.
  *Putting bad debts on the debit side. Bad debts reduce trade receivables, so they go on the **credit** side.*
* Lupa cek tak laku. Cek tak laku menambah semula hutang pelanggan, jadi di sebelah **debit** Akaun Kawalan Belum Terima.
  *Forgetting dishonoured cheques. A dishonoured cheque puts the customer's debt back, so it goes on the **debit** side.*
* Merekod kontra di sebelah yang sama dalam kedua-dua akaun kawalan.
  *Recording the contra on the same side in both control accounts.*
* Memasukkan peruntukan hutang ragu dalam akaun kawalan. Peruntukan hutang ragu tidak direkodkan dalam akaun kawalan.
  *Putting the allowance for doubtful debts in a control account. The allowance is never recorded there.*
