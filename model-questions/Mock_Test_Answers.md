**c) Explain the going concern concept and how asset values would change if a business were closing. (3)**

Going concern assumes the business will continue trading for the foreseeable future, so assets are shown at cost less depreciation. If the business were closing, assets would be shown at what they would raise if sold now, which is often much lower.
