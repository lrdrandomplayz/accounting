### 8.6 (4 marks, any two differences, 2 marks each)

* An irrecoverable debt is a **certain** loss. An allowance is an **estimate** of possible losses.
* An irrecoverable debt relates to a **named** customer. An allowance is not linked to any one customer.
* Writing off an irrecoverable debt closes the customer's account. The allowance does not change customer accounts.
* The full irrecoverable debt is an expense. Only the **change** in the allowance goes to the SPL.

**f) Hamid says: "I will make the allowance 20% next year, just to be safe." Evaluate this idea. (3)**

For: a bigger allowance is more cautious, so assets and profit are less likely to be overstated.

Against: the allowance must be a reasonable estimate based on past experience. 20% is far above Hamid's real losses, so profit and trade receivables would be understated and the statements would not be reliable. A sudden change also breaks the consistency concept.

Conclusion: keep a realistic rate (about 5%) and change it only when experience of bad debts changes. Prudence means do not overstate; it does not mean understate on purpose.

### 10.6 (4 marks)

* To match the cost of the asset against the revenue it helps earn each year. Concept: **accruals (matching)**.
* To avoid overstating the value of non-current assets and profit. Concept: **prudence**.

### 10.7 (2 marks)

### 10.8 (3 marks)

Reducing balance. A vehicle loses most value in its early years. Repairs are low when it is new and rise as it gets older. High depreciation early plus low repairs, and low depreciation later plus high repairs, spreads the total cost more evenly.

### 12.11 (3 marks)

Prudence means assets and profits should not be overstated, and liabilities and losses should not be understated. Losses are recorded as soon as they are likely; profits are recorded only when earned. Example: creating an allowance for irrecoverable debts, or valuing inventory at the lower of cost and net realisable value.

### 12.12 (3 marks)

Going concern assumes the business will continue trading for the foreseeable future, so assets are shown at cost less depreciation. If the business were **not** a going concern, assets would be shown at the amount they would raise if sold now (often much lower).

### 12.13 (4 marks, any two)

* **Relevance:** the information helps users make decisions and is available in time.
* **Reliability:** the information is free from errors and bias and can be checked.
* **Comparability:** it can be compared with earlier years and other businesses.
* **Understandability:** it is clear to users with reasonable knowledge.

### 12.14 (3 marks)

NRV = 1,800 - 300 = 1,500. Cost = 2,000. Lower of cost and NRV = **RM1,500**. Concept: **prudence**.
